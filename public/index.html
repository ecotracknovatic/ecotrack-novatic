The Problem

Tree planting projects often lack reliable data, making it difficult to prove impact, secure funding, and track long-term success.

  • Unverified planting locations
  • No standardized monitoring
  • Lack of real-time insights
  • Hard to generate credible reports

Meet EcoTrack Novatic

EcoTrack Novatic simplifies the entire lifecycle of tree planting projects with digital tools built for accuracy and transparency.

  • GPS-verified planting records
  • Photo evidence & field monitoring
  • Real-time dashboards & analytics
  • Automated reports for donors & partners
How It Works

From first seedling to final report

Six stages, one continuous, verifiable record of every project.

1

Create Organization

Set up your organization profile and invite your team, field officers, and partners.

2

Create Project

Define your project goals, site location, budget, and expected timeline.

3

Record Tree Planting

Field teams log every tree with GPS coordinates and photo evidence, online or offline.

4

Monitor Growth

Track survival, health, and maintenance activity for every tree over time.

5

AI Verification

AI cross-checks GPS data, timestamps, and photos to confirm what was actually planted.

6

Generate Professional Reports

Export donor-ready, ESG-ready reports with maps, charts, and verification records.
